# OpenPers v1.6 – Company setup guide and company documents

## Goal

After registering a new company, admins are guided through setting OpenPers up: company data,
locations, departments and teams, roles and access to sensitive data, company documents and the
first employees. The guide can be left and resumed; the dashboard shows progress until it is done
or dismissed.

## Setup guide

Steps (fixed order): `company`, `locations`, `departments`, `roles`, `documents`, `people`.

- A step is **done** when its data exists (`locations`: ≥1 workplace, `departments`: ≥1 org unit,
  `documents`: ≥1 company document, `people`: ≥2 persons) or when it was confirmed explicitly
  (`company`, `roles` only become done by confirmation – their data always exists).
- A step can be **skipped** and **reopened**. State lives in `company.settings.setupGuide`
  (`{ confirmed: string[], skipped: string[], dismissedAt?: string }`).
- API (`settings.manage`): `GET /api/v1/setup-guide` → `{ steps: [{ key, status: open|done|skipped, count? }], completed, total, dismissed }`;
  `POST /api/v1/setup-guide/steps/:key` `{ status: done|skipped|open }`; `POST /api/v1/setup-guide/dismiss`.
- UI: `/welcome` wizard with a step list; the setup wizard redirects there after creating the
  company. Dashboard card for admins with progress and "continue" until all steps are done/skipped
  or the guide is dismissed.

### Wizard content

1. **Company** – name, federal state (holidays), time zone, logo.
2. **Locations** – list + add (name, region, address).
3. **Departments & teams** – suggestions as toggle chips (Geschäftsführung, People & Culture,
   Finanzen, Vertrieb, Marketing, Produkt, Entwicklung, Kundenservice, Operations, IT), custom
   entries, teams below a department.
4. **Roles & access** – plain-language explanation of the built-in roles and four questions that
   set the access matrix: managers see compensation of their team; managers see personal data of
   their team; managers see bank & tax data; employees edit their own personal data. Link to the
   full matrix.
5. **Company documents** – recommended slots for DACH (privacy notice for employees Art. 13 GDPR,
   employee handbook / company rules, works agreements, occupational safety briefing, code of
   conduct, IT & data protection policy, travel expense policy) + custom uploads; per document
   "read confirmation required".
6. **First employees** – quick add rows (name, e-mail, position, department, start date). User
   accounts are created later in the profile (no e-mail delivery yet).

## Company documents (new module `companydocs`)

- `company_documents`: title, description, file (content-addressed storage, signature-checked
  like person documents), `requiresAck`, optional `orgUnitId` audience, uploader, timestamps.
- `company_document_acks`: (documentId, personId) primary key, `acknowledgedAt`.
- Visible to every signed-in user linked to a person in the audience; managed with
  `documents.manage`.
- `GET /api/v1/company-documents` (own ack state; for managers: ack count/audience size),
  `POST` multipart upload, `PATCH`, `DELETE`, `GET /:id/download`, `POST /:id/acknowledge`,
  `GET /:id/acknowledgements` (manage: who has/has not confirmed).
- Uploading a document that requires confirmation notifies the audience (`company_document`).
- "My documents" shows a *Company documents* section with "Gelesen & verstanden"; the nav badge
  counts open confirmations.
- GDPR: acknowledgements are exported with the person and deleted on anonymization.
